🔥 Tummy Tightener · ~12 min
Add after Mon, Wed & Fri workouts (3×/week) · 2–3 rounds
  • Hollow body hold 30s
  • Reverse crunches 12 (slow, curl hips up)
  • Dead bugs holding a 10 lb dumbbell 10/side
  • Side plank with hip dips 10/side
  • Kettlebell (15 lb) Russian twists 20
  • Plank shoulder taps 20
  • Banded Pallof press 10/side
Tips
  • Rest 45–60s between rounds
  • Exhale and pull your belly button in on every rep
  • Add a round or a heavier weight when it gets easy
  • A tighter waist also comes from protein, sleep and steps 💛
