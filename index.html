💪 Strong for My Kids

5 days/week · ~45 min
Finish every session with 10–20 min on the walking pad. Aim for 7–8k steps a day. Pick weights where the last 2 reps feel hard.
Monday · Glutes & Hamstrings
  • Smith hip thrusts 4×10
  • Dumbbell Romanian deadlifts 3×10
  • Kettlebell swings (25 lb) 3×15
  • Banded side walks 3×20
Tuesday · Back & Shoulders + Arms
  • Dumbbell rows 4×10
  • Back machine 3×12
  • Dumbbell shoulder press 3×10
  • Lateral raises 3×12
  • Band pull-aparts 3×15
Arm finisher
  • Overhead triceps extension 3×12–15
  • Triceps kickbacks 3×12–15
  • Banded pushdowns 3×15–20
  • Incline push-ups 3×max
Wednesday · Quads & Core
  • Smith squats 4×8
  • Leg extensions 3×12
  • Goblet reverse lunges 3×10/leg
  • Dead bugs 3×10
  • Plank 3×30s
Thursday · Upper Body & Posture + Arms
  • Dumbbell chest press 3×10
  • Single-arm rows 3×10
  • Banded face pulls 3×15
  • Biceps curls 2×12
  • Triceps extensions 2×12
Arm finisher
  • Overhead triceps extension 3×12–15
  • Triceps kickbacks 3×12–15
  • Banded pushdowns 3×15–20
  • Incline push-ups 3×max
Friday · Full-Body Glutes
  • Smith split squats 3×10/leg
  • Sumo kettlebell deadlifts 3×12
  • Glute bridges 3×15
  • KB circuit ×3: swings, goblet squats, halos
🍽️ Protein ~100–110 g/day · gentle calorie deficit · goal 115–120 lb
